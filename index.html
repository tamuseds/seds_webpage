FAQ

Who can join SEDS?

TAMU SEDS connects students of all backgrounds with a passion for space exploration. We are open to all TAMU students, regardless of major. Whether you’re an engineer, scientist, or just a space enthusiast, there’s a place for you here!

What do we do?

TAMU SEDS does a bit of anything. We host a general meeting on the first Wednesday of every month, with a speaker and social. Student Astroanut Corps takes place every Thursday for lectures and workshops. Join our competition and project teams or stop by for our socials!

When and where do general meetings take place?

General meetings take place on Wednesdays, 7-9 pm! We almost always host our meetings in the JEB/CHEN building.

How can I get involved with SEDS?

You can get involved by attending our general meetings, joining a project team, participating in SAC lectures/workshops, or connecting with us on Discord. Follow us on Instagram for updates!

How do I join a project/competition team?

Follow our Instagram for updates on application deadlines! They usually open at the beginning of each semester or reach out to team leads on Discord for more information!

How do I join a Student Astronaut Corp lecture/workshop?

Simply check our Instagram/Discord for updates on the dates and times for lectures and workshops, which usually take place on Thursdays, 7-8 pm, though the location may vary! No dues required for workshops or lectures.
