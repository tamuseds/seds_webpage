    SEDS TAMU SEDS Rocket Propulsion Lab

    Howdy! We are TAMU SEDS Rocket Propulsion Lab (TSRPL), a student-led rocketry team at Texas A&M University dedicated to designing, building, and launching high-performance solid fuel rockets. Our long-term mission is to develop a rocket capable of reaching the Kármán Line—100 km above Earth—while developing our own solid rocket propulsion systems along the way. Through hands-on experience in vehicle design, manufacturing, avionics, propulsion, simulation, testing, and launch operations, TSRPL gives students of all majors and experience levels the opportunity to turn classroom knowledge into real engineering experience. From a blank sheet of paper to the launch pad, our goal is to develop the engineers, leaders, and technologies needed to push student rocketry to the edge of space.

    Team Structure

    For this 2026-2027 competition cycle, the team will be composed of roughly 20 students and is divided into 3 subteams:

    • Structural: Responsible for the design, analysis, and manufacturing of the rocket’s physical structure, including the airframe, fins, nose cone, bulkheads, and motor mounting. The team ensures the vehicle can withstand aerodynamic and launch loads throughout flight.
    • Vehicle Systems: Responsible for the design and integration of the rocket’s internal systems, including propulsion, avionics, telemetry, and recovery. The team ensures these systems operate reliably and integrate properly within the vehicle.

    Our Goal

    TSRPL’s primary goal is to design, manufacture, and launch a student-developed rocket beyond the Kármán Line. Reaching this objective requires the development of several generations of increasingly capable launch vehicles, with each serving as a platform to validate new designs and technologies. Our work includes structures and aerodynamics, avionics and flight systems, recovery systems, propulsion development, manufacturing, simulation, and ground testing. Alongside vehicle development, TSRPL is working toward the design, mixing, casting, and static-fire testing of our own solid rocket motors. Through incremental testing and flight validation, these systems will ultimately be integrated into a complete space-shot vehicle.

    Our Training Team

    New members begin their time in TSRPL on a Training Team, where they spend their first several weeks designing, building, and launching a high-powered rocket from scratch. Members gain hands-on experience with CAD, structures, aerodynamics, avionics, recovery systems, simulation, manufacturing, and launch operations while learning the engineering design process used throughout TSRPL. Training Teams provide members with the technical foundation needed to contribute to our larger vehicle and propulsion projects.

    Time Commitment

    We are holding weekly meetings for:
